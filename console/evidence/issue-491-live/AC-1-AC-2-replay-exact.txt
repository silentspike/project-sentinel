Live production daemon on the deploy VM (operator API 127.0.0.1:8084), token gate active
(gateway/judge off, 0 tokens). Binary built from fix/issue-491-anchor-tick-guard.

Method (defeats the continuous-tick race): take a fresh anchor snapshot, record the
forward ground-truth state-hash per tick, then restore {target_tick: T*} and record the
post-restore forward sequence. After the restore the daemon resumes at exactly T*+1, which
proves the world was left at T* by the bounded replay; every subsequent tick's canonical
state-hash (strict AND core) must then match the ground-truth recording byte-for-byte (a
wrong restore state diverges at the first tick). The restored target tick itself addresses
~44 replayed ticks (anchor → T*).
